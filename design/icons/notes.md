# Screen Recorder icon concepts

## Concept 1: screen with record dot

A rounded monitor with a short stand, and a red record dot with a light ring in the middle of the screen. Colors are a slate gradient from #6B7A94 to #46536A, red #E5484D, and light #F2F4F8. At 16 pixels it reads as a gray screen with a red dot in a light ring, and the stand shrinks to about 1 pixel but still hints "monitor". The mid-tone slate keeps it visible on both light and dark taskbars.

## Concept 2: region capture

Four thick rounded corner brackets frame a large red record dot, with no screen outline. Colors are slate #5E6C86 and red #E5484D. At 16 pixels it reads as a red dot inside a dotted square, the simplest and lightest of the four. It ties to the dragged-region feature and the red Record button on the toolbar.

## Concept 3: screen plus face cam

A rounded screen with a red record dot in its upper left, and a round webcam bubble with a person silhouette overlapping the bottom right corner. Colors are a slate gradient from #6B7A94 to #46536A, red #E5484D, and light #F2F4F8. At 16 pixels the screen and red dot still read, but the person inside the bubble turns into a gray blur, and the light ring around the bubble fades on a light taskbar.

## Concept 4: red tile

A solid rounded-square tile in record red, with a light rounded frame and a dot inside it, like a viewfinder. Colors are a red gradient from #F0646A to #D63A41, and light #FFF1F1. At 16 pixels it is a bold red square with a light ring and a center dot, and it stands out most in the taskbar. It looks the most like a Windows 11 tile-style icon.
